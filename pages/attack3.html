Simple Tomato Soup

This tomato soup takes about 30 minutes and uses ingredients most kitchens already have.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 800 g canned tomatoes
  • 500 ml vegetable stock
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil for about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes and stock, then simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth and season to taste.

Serve warm with bread. Leftovers keep in the fridge for three days.
